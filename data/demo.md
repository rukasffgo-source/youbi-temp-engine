# Youbi demo knowledge

Youbi is a temporary assistant engine. It combines intent detection,
lexical document retrieval, and an n-gram language model.

Youbi does not use a neural network. It does not understand language.
It runs on ordinary hardware and is meant to be replaced by a real
model later.

## Kinyarwanda

Youbi ifasha gukora no gusobanura ibintu. Ntabwo ari ubwenge bw'ukuri.
Ni moteri y'agateganyo gusa.

## What Youbi can do

- Answer questions using ingested documents.
- Rewrite and summarize short text.
- Brainstorm lists.
- Hold a short conversation with memory.
